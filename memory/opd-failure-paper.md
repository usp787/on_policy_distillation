[Rethinking On-Policy Distillation: Phenomenology, Mechanism, and Recipe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016) — THUNLP/Tsinghua (Zhiyuan Liu, Ning Ding et al.); code https://github.com/thunlp/OPD. Apr 2026 preprint — credible, methodologically rigorous (controlled teacher-swaps across DeepSeek + Qwen families, clean metrics, causal ablations, reverse-distillation), but recent and likely **not yet peer-reviewed** → treat as a strong, well-argued hypothesis, not settled fact.

Two conditions govern whether OPD helps: (i) student & teacher share compatible **thinking patterns** (high top-k token overlap on student-visited states); (ii) **higher score ≠ new knowledge** — the teacher must carry capability *beyond what the student saw in training*. A same-family teacher differing only in scale (their R1-Distill-7B→1.5B; Qwen3-4B→1.7B same pipeline) drives ~indistinguishable, often **null** distillation despite higher benchmarks — "a higher score may merely reflect a different degree of fit to the same data, rather than genuinely novel capabilities." Mechanism (§4): successful OPD = top-k overlap rising (72%→91%) + overlap-token advantage → 0 + entropy gap narrowing; failing runs **stagnate from the outset**; optimizing overlap tokens alone matches full OPD. §6: a much larger teacher can induce a **locally-flat reward landscape** around the student → token gradients ineffective despite a globally-correct signal; reward reliability also degrades with trajectory depth (teacher continuation edge +0.37 at 1K prefix → +0.02 at 16K; length sweet-spot ~3–7K for thinking models, <1K too short). Recipe (§5) to rescue *condition-(i)* failures: off-policy SFT cold-start on teacher rollouts; teacher-aligned prompt selection.

**Why it explains our [[project-progress]] Phase-2 null:** our teacher (Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507) and student (4B-Instruct-2507) are same-family, same non-thinking mode, same 2507 release → condition (i) satisfied but (ii) violated → the +14 AIME gap was "better fit," not transferable new knowledge → no gain, their exact "strong teacher, tiny gap-recovery" pattern. Refines our earlier read: **dominant cause = teacher choice (no new knowledge)**, with our 2048-cap + LoRA-r32 as secondary confounds (their clean-setup failures argue the teacher explanation stands on its own). Also vindicates dropping SFT — cold-start fixes pattern-mismatch we didn't have. To confirm in our run: measure top-k overlap ratio + overlap-token advantage between our 4B and 30B on student rollouts (predict high overlap, stagnant advantage). Caveat: their core experiments are thinking-mode reasoning models; ours is non-thinking instruct + noisier eval (60Q avg@4), so the mapping was by-analogy until measured.

**MEASURED (2026-06, src/diag_overlap.py, fresh 4B vs 30B-FP8, 64 rollouts / ~57k student-visited positions):** overlap_ratio **0.672** (HIGH → condition (i) satisfied, SFT cold-start wouldn't help), entropy_gap **0.092** nats (both entropies ~0.20 → near-identical peaked confidence), mass on shared tokens **~0.998** (the +14 edge barely surfaces as a different token-level distribution on-policy), overlap_advantage **−0.152** (NOT near-zero — a real residual ~0.15-nat reverse-KL on the overlap set, comparable to the entropy itself; the script's canned "near-zero" line oversold it). Read: strongly confirms (i) is fine and is consistent with (ii) as the blocker, BUT honest caveats = the residual signal is non-trivial (just diluted across ~99.8% agreement) and this is a single snapshot, not the train-dynamics curve → corroboration, not proof.
